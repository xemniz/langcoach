# LangCoach

Individual language tuition for learners ranging from complete beginners to advanced speakers.

## Language

**Learner**:
The person receiving language instruction, with their own reasons for learning and current abilities.
_Avoid_: User when discussing learning rather than app operation.

**AI teacher**:
The language tutor intended to provide individual instruction appropriate to the learner's abilities.
_Avoid_: Conversation partner when referring to the full teaching role.

**Lesson**:
An hour-long period of individual language instruction between a learner and the AI teacher.
_Avoid_: Chat when referring to the teaching unit.

**Practical goal**:
A real-world use of the target language that the teacher infers from conversation with the learner.
_Avoid_: Topic when referring to the purpose for learning.
