# 04: Teach one prepared lesson

**What to build:** Prepare and conduct a flexible hour-long lesson with one objective, suitable material, explanation or diagnosis, meaningful practice, focused feedback, a further attempt, and an evidence-based close.

**Blocked by:** 01: Discover and confirm practical goals; 03: Record demonstrated learning accurately.

**Status:** completed

- [x] The teacher begins with a prepared direction and follows a coherent lesson arc without rigid minute-by-minute scripting.
- [x] Feedback leads to another meaningful attempt when appropriate.
- [x] Ending early records only what was observed.
